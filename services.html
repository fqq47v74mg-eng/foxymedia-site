Measurement

One identifier, click to ledger.

We do not run two sets of books. The click identifier issued at the placement is the same one that appears against the revenue event in your system, which is what makes a dispute a five-minute query instead of a month-end argument.

  • S2S postback, pixel fallback, or scheduled API pull from your CRM
  • Signed requests — HMAC on every inbound event
  • Sub-ID taxonomy agreed before launch (source, placement, creative, angle)
  • Deduplication window and rejection reasons defined in the IO
  • Read-only dashboard access for your analysts

Questions we get early

Straight answers.

What is the minimum test budget?

It depends on the target cost per event, not on a fixed floor: we need roughly 30–50 qualified events to read a source honestly. For a $200 CPA that is a $6k–$10k test; for a $40 CPL it is far less. We will size it with you on the first call.

Do you work with unlicensed operators?

No. We ask for licence details, target-market restrictions and the entity that will be invoiced before we scope. In several categories we also require proof of the payment arrangement, because a funnel that cannot take payment cannot convert.

Will we see the actual placements?

Yes. Advertisers get source and placement-level reporting. Where a publisher has a commercial reason to be masked, they are identified by a stable ID you can block, whitelist or negotiate against.

Can you work with our existing tracker?

Usually. We integrate with the mainstream affiliate platforms and with direct APIs. If your stack is bespoke, we will write to your spec — the requirement is a unique click identifier and a server-side event we can be signalled on.

Who owns the creative?

You do, for anything produced for your account. Pre-landers built on our own properties stay with us, but the angles, tests and results are shared with you in full.

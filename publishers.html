Traffic we accept

What works here.

  • SEO, comparison and review sites with verifiable rankings
  • Paid social and native media buyers with their own creative
  • Email and CRM databases with documented consent
  • Telegram, Discord and community traffic with engagement history
  • App and push inventory with transparent sub-source reporting

What gets you removed

And what does not work.

  • Incentivised or misrepresented traffic on non-incent offers
  • Brand bidding or trademark abuse against the advertiser
  • Cookie stuffing, forced clicks, bot or emulator traffic
  • Undisclosed sub-affiliates or rebrokered volume
  • Claims that breach the advertiser’s licence conditions

Joining

Four steps, usually under 48 hours.

STEP 01

Apply

Send your traffic sources, the verticals and GEOs you run, and your monthly volume. Existing network references help.

STEP 02

Verification

We check ownership of the traffic, past compliance history and, where relevant, sample conversions. This is why our advertisers pay premium rates.

STEP 03

Terms

Payout, cap, hold period and payment rail agreed in writing, plus tracking links and postback setup.

STEP 04

Scale

Deliver clean volume and payouts move up. We would rather raise your rate than lose the source to a competitor.

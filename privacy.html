Privacy policy

How FoxyMedia Ltd collects, uses and protects personal data, in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Last updated: 2026

This page is a starting template. Have it reviewed by counsel before launch — the wording below is not legal advice.

Who we are

FoxyMedia Ltd, registered in England and Wales, is the data controller for personal data collected through foxymedia.io. Contact privacy@foxymedia.io for any request under this policy.

What we collect

Contact details you submit through our forms (name, company, email, messaging handle, and the content of your enquiry); business data needed to onboard advertisers and publishers; and technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, and pages visited.

Campaign and tracking data

In the course of delivering campaigns we process click and conversion identifiers, coarse geolocation, device signals and event data supplied by advertisers. Where we act on an advertiser’s instruction we do so as a processor, governed by the data processing terms in the relevant insertion order.

Why we process it

To respond to enquiries and provide our services (contract); to detect and prevent advertising fraud, and to measure and improve campaign performance (legitimate interests); to meet legal, accounting and anti-money-laundering obligations (legal obligation); and to send commercial updates where you have opted in (consent).

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to operate the site, and analytics and attribution cookies where you consent. You can withdraw consent at any time through your browser settings or the cookie controls on this site. Third-party ad platforms we work with may set their own cookies on pages where their tags are deployed.

Sharing

We share data with advertisers and publishers where necessary to deliver and verify a campaign, with technology providers acting on our behalf (hosting, tracking, CRM, anti-fraud), and with professional advisers or authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

International transfers

Where data moves outside the UK or EEA we rely on adequacy regulations or on standard contractual clauses with appropriate supplementary measures.

Retention

Enquiry data is kept for 24 months from last contact. Campaign and financial records are kept for six years to meet statutory accounting and audit requirements.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Write to privacy@foxymedia.io. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

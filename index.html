How an account runs

From scoped brief to scaled spend in under 30 days.

STEP 01

Scope

We agree the target event, the acceptable cost per event, the GEOs and the licence constraints. If the economics do not work, we say so on the first call rather than sell a retainer.

STEP 02

Integrate

Tracking goes in before budget does: postback or API, test conversions verified end to end, sub-ID taxonomy agreed so every report reconciles with yours.

STEP 03

Test

A controlled budget across a shortlist of sources and angles. Two to three weeks, enough volume to reach significance on the event you pay for — not on clicks.

STEP 04

Scale

Winning sources get budget and caps raised weekly. Losing sources are cut, not “optimised”. Publisher payouts are repriced against realised value.

STEP 05

Report

Live dashboard access plus a weekly written read on margin, cohort payback and what we intend to change. Quarterly commercial review.

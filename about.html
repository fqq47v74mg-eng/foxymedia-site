Who we are

Four years running affiliate and media for an iGaming operator. Now running it for clients.

FoxyMedia was founded by Omer Cohen, who spent four years as CMO at a European iGaming operator managing the full performance marketing stack: affiliate recruitment and account management, paid media across native and push, SEO and content, CRM integration and conversion tracking. At peak, the programme ran seven figures in annual media spend across 30+ GEOs and a roster of 200+ active publishers.

That background is why FoxyMedia is built the way it is. We know what a €200 CPA looks like from the operator side, which sources actually hold LTV at week four, and why the cheapest lead in a regulated category is almost never the right one. We now bring that operator perspective to clients who want better outcomes than a traditional agency can deliver — and we carry enough of the risk to have skin in the same game.

How we operate

Four commitments we will hold you to as well.

Price the truth

A source is worth what it produces downstream, not what it promised at click. We reprice weekly and we show our working — including when it moves against us.

Compliance is commercial

In licensed categories, a creative that cannot be approved is a creative that cannot be scaled. Legal review sits inside the media team, not in a queue after launch.

No blind bundles

Advertisers see the placements their budget bought. Publishers see the advertiser their traffic served. Neither side is asked to trust an aggregate.

Say no early

We turn down accounts where the target cost per event is not achievable in the GEOs requested. A declined brief costs a week; a failed campaign costs a quarter.

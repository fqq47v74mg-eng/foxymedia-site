A media company that thinks like a trading desk.

FoxyMedia is a UK-based digital agency built around one idea: traffic is an asset with a price, and the party who measures it best should be the one who buys it.

Who we are

Built by operators from payments, brokerage and affiliate marketing.

The team came out of B2B foreign exchange, payment operations and performance marketing — people who have sat on the receivables side of a high-risk merchant account and on the buying side of a nine-figure media plan. That background is why our reporting reconciles to a ledger rather than to a dashboard screenshot.

We run as a principal, not only as an agent. Where the numbers support it, FoxyMedia buys media on its own book and sells qualified outcomes to advertisers — which means our incentive is the same as yours: volume that survives the finance review.

How we operate

Four commitments we will hold you to as well.

Price the truth

A source is worth what it produces downstream, not what it promised at click. We reprice weekly and we show our working — including when it moves against us.

Compliance is commercial

In licensed categories, a creative that cannot be approved is a creative that cannot be scaled. Legal review sits inside the media team, not in a queue after launch.

No blind bundles

Advertisers see the placements their budget bought. Publishers see the advertiser their traffic served. Neither side is asked to trust an aggregate.

Say no early

We turn down accounts where the target cost per event is not achievable in the GEOs requested. A declined brief costs a week; a failed campaign costs a quarter.

A performance agency for regulated markets.

FoxyMedia is a London-based performance marketing agency and publisher network. We help licensed brands in finance, iGaming and other regulated categories acquire customers profitably — and pay publishers fairly for the traffic that gets them there.

Who we are

An operator’s view of acquisition.

We run the full performance stack in-house: affiliate recruitment and management, paid media, SEO and content, CRM integration and conversion tracking. Every source is priced on what it produces downstream, not on what it promised at click.

That means we know what a sustainable CPA looks like, which sources hold their value over time, and why the cheapest lead in a regulated category is rarely the right one.

How we operate

Four commitments.

Price the truth

A source is worth what it produces downstream. We reprice weekly and show our working — including when it moves against us.

Compliance is commercial

A creative that can’t be approved can’t be scaled. Legal review sits inside the media team, not in a queue after launch.

No blind bundles

Advertisers see the placements their budget bought. Publishers see the advertiser their traffic served.

Say no early

If the target cost per event isn’t achievable in the GEOs requested, we tell you before you spend anything.

The team

Small, senior and reachable.

Accounts are run by the people who scoped them — no handover from a pitch team to a junior desk. Your commercial lead answers their own messages and owns the number your campaign is measured on.

  • Commercial — advertiser strategy, deal structure, market entry
  • Media — buying, creative and optimisation across paid and affiliate
  • Technology — tracking, BI, anti-fraud and integrations
  • Compliance — licence mapping, creative review, publisher vetting

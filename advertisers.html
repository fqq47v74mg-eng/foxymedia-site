Media plan in five days

Placement transparency

Integration

Tracking goes in before budget does.

We will not launch against an unverified integration. Your team fires a test event, we confirm attribution end to end, and only then does spend start.

  • Standard platforms: Affise, Everflow, Voluum, HasOffers, Trackier
  • Direct API and webhook integrations for bespoke backends
  • HMAC-signed postbacks and an agreed deduplication window
  • Rejection reasons enumerated in the insertion order

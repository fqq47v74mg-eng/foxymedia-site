Why high-risk is different

Three constraints decide the media plan before creative does.

In an unregulated category you buy the cheapest qualified traffic. In ours, the cheapest source is frequently the one that cannot be used — and finding that out after launch is expensive.

Licence and territory

What the operator is permitted to promote, to whom, and in which language — enforced at placement and creative level, not just in the terms.

Platform policy

Ad networks, app stores and search engines each restrict these categories differently, and change the rules without notice. Our buying mix assumes that.

Payment rails

A funnel converts only as well as it can be paid for. We plan around acquiring, local methods and chargeback exposure in each GEO.

Thirteen categories where the compliance work is the moat.

Each of these markets has its own licence regime, platform policy and payment reality. We keep a live map of all three, per GEO, because that is what decides whether a campaign can scale or merely launch.

01

Pharma & Nutra

Licensed pharmacy, telehealth and supplement brands face creative restrictions on every major ad network. We run compliant acquisition across placements that accept the category, with health claims reviewed against MHRA guidelines and the target market’s advertising code before a creative goes live.

02

FX & CFD

FCA, CySEC and ASIC-regulated broker acquisition requires licence-specific risk warning copy, GEO-level eligibility rules and event pricing on funded account rather than registration. We run the full funnel: lead qualification, KYC warm-up and first-deposit attribution across tier-1 and emerging markets.

03

Insurance

Motor, health, travel and life comparison campaigns where pricing on a completed quote or policy issue beats cost-per-click by an order of magnitude. We run call-tracking, duplicate suppression and FCA-compliant creative for UK and EU licensed carriers.

04

Trading & Investing

Equities, futures, prop-firm challenge funnels and investment platform acquisition. Campaigns are optimised on deposit or funded challenge, not on click-to-registration — because in this category, registration volume and real user quality have almost no correlation.

05

Crypto & Web3

Crypto exchange, wallet, infrastructure and DeFi campaigns are restricted or banned outright on most self-serve ad platforms. We buy through networks and direct inventory that consistently pass creative review, and price campaigns on KYC pass or first trade rather than install or click.

06

iGaming Affiliate

Our deepest vertical. Casino and sportsbook operator campaigns run on CPA, RevShare or hybrid, with first-deposit reporting, player LTV cohorts and bonus-abuse detection built into the attribution. Licence coverage spans MGA, UKGC, Curacao and emerging-market operators.

07

Brokerage

Introducing-broker and white-label partner recruitment for multi-asset and niche brokers. We run demand-generation campaigns priced on a qualified partner inquiry or signed agreement, not on impressions — across EMEA, LATAM and APAC markets with market-specific lead qualification.

08

Payment Processing

Merchant acquiring, PSP, orchestration and alternative payments demand-generation. High-risk merchants are notoriously hard to reach through conventional B2B channels — we run targeted outreach through publications, communities and networks where that audience actually makes decisions.

09

HR & Recruitment

Job-board, ATS, EOR and staffing platform lead generation. We separate employer demand from candidate supply and price on a completed employer account setup or a qualified vacancy brief — not on clicks from job seekers who were never the target audience.

10

Incorporation

Company formation, nominee and corporate services across offshore and onshore jurisdictions. This category sits in a compliance grey zone on most platforms — we run it through placements with long-established histories in the space and creative that passes scrutiny in every target market.

11

Digital Banking

Neobank, IBAN and multi-currency account acquisition measured on KYC pass and first transaction, not on sign-ups. The conversion gap between registration and a funded account is where most digital banking campaigns leak — our funnel attribution surfaces that at the source level so we cut the leakers before month end.

12

Legal Services

Claims, immigration and corporate legal intake with pre-qualification built into the funnel. We gate on jurisdiction, claim type and basic eligibility before a lead reaches a fee earner — because an unqualified legal inquiry costs the firm the same as a billable one, just without the revenue.

13

E-commerce

Marketplace sellers and DTC brands that need performance buying beyond Meta and Google. We run native, push and programmatic with feed-level bid control, creative rotation by product category and a margin-first view on ROAS that accounts for returns and fulfilment cost.

Why high-risk is different

Three constraints decide the media plan before creative does.

In an unregulated category you buy the cheapest qualified traffic. In ours, the cheapest source is frequently the one that cannot be used — and finding that out after launch is expensive.

Licence and territory

What the operator is permitted to promote, to whom, and in which language — enforced at placement and creative level, not just in the terms.

Platform policy

Ad networks, app stores and search engines each restrict these categories differently, and change the rules without notice. Our buying mix assumes that.

Payment rails

A funnel converts only as well as it can be paid for. We plan around acquiring, local methods and chargeback exposure in each GEO.
